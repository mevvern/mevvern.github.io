THERE ARE TIMES WHEN I TRULY CONSIDER WHAT YOU MEAN AND WHAT YOU ARE.

INVARIABLY, THEN, THERE ARE TIMES WHEN I FEEL THAT YOU EXIST NOT TO LIVE YOUR OWN LIFE;

THAT YOU LIVE INSTEAD TO SOOTHE A RAGGED, INFLAMED WOUND TO MY TO MINE.

YOU WERE NEVER MY SALVE. YOU DO NOT SERVE TO HEAL ME. YOUR PURPOSE IS YOUR OWN.

I ADORE YOUR APPEARANCES. I ABHOR YOUR ABSENCES.

THERE IS A VOID IN MY MIND.

IT IS NOT YOURS TO FILL.
